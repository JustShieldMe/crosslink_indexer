A bond's amount stops changing when it leaves the Active state, so for every bond accrued = value − principal. Accrued across all bonds equals staking issuance exactly.

How the figures were checked

Staking rewards are state, so they cannot be read from transactions. The replay feeds every indexed staking action into zebra_state::StakingReplay, which applies each block's actions in transaction order, then the 5 ctaz reward, then any slash burns. The results were then checked against the running node.

Decisions for the reward process

The protocol paid staking rewards by bond size alone. These are the places where that may not match what the team intends to reward. The IDs are for reference in discussion.

D1

Burned bonds: 401,020 ctaz across 341 bonds

Three social slashes burned every bond that pointed at a terminated finalizer at any point in a window before activation, including bonds that retargeted or unbonded away inside the window. Most of the burned value is staking reward accrued before the burn.

PoW 225,000 · 1 finalizer · 159 bonds · 189,458.71 ctaz
PoW 655,350 · 17 finalizers · 100 bonds · 61,941.24 ctaz
PoW 658,200 · 3 finalizers · 82 bonds · 149,620.05 ctaz

The 658,200 rule burned 81,736.24 ctaz on 22d586eba970a072…, a finalizer that signed 54.6% of the BFT blocks it was eligible for. Its delegators may not have had reason to expect a slash.

D2

Rewards ignored finalizer performance

Issuance does not look at which finalizer a bond targets or whether that finalizer signs. Delegators to a finalizer that never signed earned the same rate as delegators to the most reliable one. Sign rates are in the finalizer table if the team wants to weight by them. At the cutoff, only one finalizer with active stake signs less than half the time: 2cf7e8bda2dc6590… at 40.5% with 85,379.08 ctaz.

D3

Mirror keys: stake delegated to byte-reversed finalizer keys

17 roster keys are the exact byte-reversal of a real, signing finalizer's key. None of them has ever signed a BFT block. This is consistent with delegation tooling that serialized the target key in the wrong byte order. Stake on these keys earned full rewards while adding no voting power to the finalizer it was meant for.

The largest case: 034ad1af698987ba…, the mirror of the top finalizer, held a mean of 107,092 ctaz of roster power over 111,827 eligible BFT heights and signed nothing. By 660,000 almost all such stake had been retargeted (321 bonds were retargeted in total); 134.54 ctaz still sat on mirror keys, most of it burned.

D4

Stake cannot be tied to wallets from chain data

Bonds are created by shielded transactions. The chain records a bond key and a target finalizer, not the wallet that funded the bond, and there is no separate finalizer reward: an operator's staking income is the bonds they created. Mining is different: coinbase outputs go to transparent addresses.

Two ways to attribute: participants submit a UFVK and the team runs the node's get_total_issuance(ufvks, 1, 660000), which uses the same StakingReplay and so reproduces these figures per wallet; or participants prove ownership of bond keys and miner addresses by signing a challenge.

D5

Withdrawn and unbonding bonds

10 withdrawn bonds (834.54 ctaz) have already paid out their principal and rewards to their owners. 2 bonds (93.18 ctaz) were mid-unbonding at the cutoff. Decide whether realized rewards count toward the allocation.

D6

Cutoff and schedule

Figures include block 660,000's staking reward. The BFT counterpart is height 126,209, the last BFT block finalizing a PoW block at or below the cutoff. Burns follow the node's current three-rule schedule; the replay reads the schedule from zebrad.toml, so if the adopted schedule changes, rerun it rather than adjusting figures by hand.

Bond lookup

Search all 10,737 bonds by bond key or by target finalizer (at least 4 hex characters). Bond keys are raw arg32_0 bytes, the form getbondinfo accepts. The full table is in bonds.csv.

Data and reproduction

Everything is in ~/crosslink-indexer/reports/pow-660000/ on the node host.

bonds.csvOne row per bond: key, current and original target, created height, principal, value, accrued, status and the height it changed.
finalizers.csvPer finalizer: termination, roster presence, BFT signing, bond counts, active, accrued and burned value.
miners.csvPer miner address: blocks, share, height range, subsidy, fees and total.
replay_staking_rewards.rsThe replay tool, a zebra-crosslink example built on zebra_state::StakingReplay.
analyze.pyJoins the replay, BFT participation and mining into the tables above.
crosslink-indexer --db rewards_660k.sqlite pow --to 660000
crosslink-indexer --db bft_now.sqlite bft --pos-chain <cache>/pos.chain
sqlite3 -separator $'\t' rewards_660k.sqlite "SELECT height, tx_index, kind_name, amount_zats,
  lower(hex(bond_key)), lower(hex(coalesce(target_finalizer, x''))) FROM staking_action
  ORDER BY height, tx_index" > actions.tsv
cargo run --release -p zebra-crosslink --example replay_staking_rewards -- \
  actions.tsv ~/.config/zebrad.toml 660000 bonds.csv
python3 analyze.py
